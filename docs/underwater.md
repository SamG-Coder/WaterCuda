# Underwater reefs

The browser and native viewer compile the same terrain and rendering `.cu` files. Host code only manages buffers, camera controls and dispatches. Use `?seed=884&view=reef&look=coastal` locally or native `--view reef`; key 7 opens this preset.

The ocean floor connects the island shelves with basin, ridge and shelf noise. A 600 m seeded regional field leaves broad reef-free areas. Within eligible regions, 120 m cells may contain irregular outcrops approximately 48-104 m across. Cells can be empty; individual coral families and colours have their own seeds. Integer lattice hashing preserves the world under floating-origin rebasing.

Depth is measured from the local base seabed below mean sea level. Living cover ramps up between 3 and 8 m, is strongest through the shallower shelf, fades from 30 to 55 m, and is absent beyond 55 m. Between 18 and 42 m, the mix increasingly favours plates and encrusting forms and reduces colony height. These are artistic habitat rules, not a biological population model. Reef relief is capped below mean sea level.

A moving 1024-square GPU cache stores the rock foundation and substrate colour over 384 m. A second bounded cache stores 160-square colony descriptors. Both rebuild on 12 m camera steps, seed changes or origin changes. Living colonies use analytical capsule/ellipsoid intersections with branches, gaps, plate undersides and self-shadowing, rather than terrain displacement. Integer DDA traversal explicitly advances cell indices to avoid missing cells when a ray step is smaller than coordinate precision. Optical visibility extends to 150 m and fades into water-column scattering.

## Single specimen study

Placement is currently deferred while the coral asset is refined. Open `?seed=884&view=coral&look=coastal`, press **8**, or choose **Coral study**. Native: `--view coral`. This isolates one pink branching specimen with 29 spreading arms, secondary forks, finer twigs, pale tips and fine surface bump detail. It stands on a small rock with a simple seabed backdrop. All intersections, materials and lighting remain in the shared `.cu` source. Study mode bypasses the world reef cache and starts at a slower inspection speed.

This is a procedural specimen prototype. It does not yet reproduce all the complexity of the photographic reference, and it has no fish or volumetric shafts. The broad reef placement views remain experimental; they are not the approved coral asset or final distribution.

Growth-form references: [NOAA coral growth forms](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/education/tutorial_corals/coral03_growth.html), [AIMS table corals](https://www.aims.gov.au/information-centre/news-and-stories/turning-tables-how-table-corals-are-regenerating-reefs-decades-faster-any-other-coral-type). No downloaded texture or model is used.

Validation: `npm test`, `npm run test:native`, and `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File native/build.ps1 -Test`. Tests cover depth exclusion, sparse versus dense habitat, seeded morphology, remote rebasing, cell continuity, cache lattice stability, cache invalidation and traversal versus a dense reference. Native timings are GPU render timings, not browser FPS or total display latency.

## Branching family and detail budgets

`?seed=884&view=family&look=coastal` shows three related variants, generated by the same branching rules as the detailed study specimen. Native: `--view family`. They share the pink/pale-tip material; seeds change arm reach, height and forks. The world seed controls the family deterministically. This is an inspection layout, not new reef placement.

The reference study uses 29 arms / 493 branch capsules at full detail. Family members start at 17 arms / 289 capsules, then use projected pixel footprint in colony-local units:

| Level | Footprint | Capsules per family member |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Close | up to 0.012 | 289 |
| Medium | up to 0.028 | 153 |
| Reduced tips | up to 0.065 | 85 |
| Far | up to 0.15 | 27 |
| Very far | above 0.15 | 15 |

These are capsule counts, not mesh vertices or timing guarantees. Each capsule has a cylinder and rounded end tests. Coarser levels retain a subset of the same seeded skeleton and widen retained branches to compensate for lost coverage. The colony is not culled by LOD. Normal/material detail also filters with pixel footprint. Shadows use a cheaper footprint than close primary visibility. Transitions currently use discrete topology levels; this is not a continuous mesh morph or an impostor atlas.

Tests compare silhouette coverage across all detail levels and verify that different family seeds change visible geometry. The existing screen-resolution selector also affects footprint and therefore geometry detail automatically.
